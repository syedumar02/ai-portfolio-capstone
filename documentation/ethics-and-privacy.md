# Ethics, Privacy, Copyright, and AI Limitations

**Student:** Syed Umar T  
**Course:** B.Tech Information Technology — Second Year  
**Project:** AI Portfolio Website Content Creator

## 1. Introduction

This project explores the use of artificial intelligence to assist with personal portfolio content creation. AI can improve writing and support design, but it can also produce inaccurate information, generic content, or material that raises privacy and copyright concerns.

Responsible use requires human review, accurate representation, and respect for the rights of others.

## 2. Accuracy and Honesty

AI-generated content must not misrepresent the student's experience or abilities.

The following practices will be followed:
- List only skills that I actually possess.
- Do not invent achievements, certifications, or employment experience.
- Distinguish completed projects from planned work.
- Verify factual claims before publishing.
- Correct or remove unsupported statements.

## 3. Privacy and Personal Information

A public portfolio may be viewed by anyone on the internet.

Privacy precautions include:
- Avoid publishing a home address, phone number, passwords, or sensitive personal information.
- Review AI tool privacy settings and applicable policies.
- Avoid submitting confidential information to AI tools.
- Use only appropriate professional contact information.
- Check screenshots and uploaded files for accidentally exposed information.

## 4. Copyright and Intellectual Property

Portfolio text, images, icons, fonts, and other assets must be used responsibly.

The following precautions will be taken:
- Prefer original graphics or assets with suitable usage permissions.
- Review the licence and terms of any third-party asset.
- Attribute sources when required.
- Do not assume that AI-generated content is automatically free of copyright concerns.
- Keep records of the sources and licences for published assets.

## 5. Bias and Fairness

AI-generated content can reflect biases or make assumptions about a person's background, abilities, or career path.

To reduce these risks:
- Provide accurate context in prompts.
- Review generated language for stereotypes and unfair assumptions.
- Use inclusive and professional wording.
- Retain control over final content decisions.

## 6. AI Limitations

AI tools may:
- Generate inaccurate or unsupported claims.
- Produce repetitive or generic wording.
- Misunderstand instructions.
- Suggest unsuitable images or design choices.
- Provide outdated information.
- Miss errors that require human judgment.

AI output will therefore be treated as a draft, not as automatically verified information.

## 7. Transparency

AI tools may be used for drafting, proofreading, idea generation, or visual design. Their actual contributions will be documented in the project report.

The final content will be reviewed and edited to ensure that it accurately represents my skills, interests, and work.

## 8. Human Responsibility

I remain responsible for the content published on my website. AI assistance does not replace my responsibility to verify information, respect privacy, check licensing conditions, and correct mistakes.

## 9. Conclusion

Responsible AI use requires accuracy, transparency, privacy protection, copyright awareness, and human oversight. Applying these principles helps make the portfolio more trustworthy and professionally appropriate.
