# Ethics, Privacy, Copyright and AI Limitations

**Student:** Syed Umar T  
**Course:** B.Tech Information Technology — Second Year  
**Project:** AI Portfolio Website Content Creator

## 1. Introduction

This project uses AI assistance to support the creation and review of personal portfolio content. Responsible use requires accurate information, human oversight, privacy protection, and respect for intellectual property.

## 2. Accuracy and Honesty

AI-generated content may contain incorrect information or unsupported claims.

To reduce this risk:
- Verify education, skills, and project details before publication.
- Do not invent qualifications, work experience, awards, or achievements.
- Distinguish completed activities from planned activities.
- Correct or remove claims that cannot be supported by evidence.
- Review the final website personally before publishing.

## 3. Privacy and Personal Information

A portfolio should contain only information appropriate for public viewing.

Privacy precautions include:
- Do not publish passwords, account credentials, private addresses, or sensitive personal details.
- Avoid submitting confidential information to AI services.
- Review the information included in prompts before sending them to an AI tool.
- Check the privacy settings and data-handling policies of the services used.
- Share contact details only when necessary and appropriate.

## 4. Copyright and Intellectual Property

AI-generated text and images may raise copyright, licensing, or attribution questions.

The project should:
- Use original content wherever possible.
- Review the licensing terms of images, fonts, icons, and other assets.
- Avoid copying protected content without permission.
- Provide attribution when required by a licence or source.
- Keep records of the sources and tools used to create visual assets.
- Review the applicable terms of AI tools before publishing their outputs.

The use of an AI tool does not automatically establish that every output is free of copyright restrictions.

## 5. Bias and Fairness

AI-generated writing may rely on generic assumptions about students, careers, or technical ability.

To reduce this risk:
- Use personal facts rather than stereotypes.
- Avoid exaggerated descriptions of technical ability.
- Review suggestions for misleading or inappropriate assumptions.
- Keep the final wording representative of the student's actual background.

## 6. Human Oversight

AI is used as an assistant, not as the final authority.

Human review is required to:
- Check factual accuracy.
- Select and revise useful suggestions.
- Reject unsuitable or misleading output.
- Test website functionality.
- Approve the final content before publication.

## 7. Limitations of AI Tools

AI tools can produce:
- Incorrect or unverifiable claims.
- Generic or repetitive wording.
- Suggestions that do not match the intended audience.
- Inconsistent answers to similar prompts.
- Content that requires substantial human editing.

AI services may also have usage limits, changing features, or different terms of service.

## 8. Risk and Mitigation Table

| Risk | Potential impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Incorrect AI-generated facts | Misleading portfolio content | Verify claims before publication |
| Exaggerated skills | Misrepresentation of ability | Describe only actual skills and experience |
| Exposure of personal data | Privacy risk | Minimize personal information in prompts and public pages |
| Unlicensed visual assets | Copyright or licensing concerns | Check permissions and attribution requirements |
| Generic AI writing | Weak personal identity | Edit content to reflect real interests and goals |
| Untested website features | Broken links or layout problems | Perform and record functionality tests |

## 9. Transparency and Documentation

The project documentation should identify which AI tools were actually used, the tasks they supported, and the corrections made to their outputs.

Planned activities must not be presented as completed work. Evidence should be retained for important prompts, revisions, verification checks, and reviewer feedback.

## 10. Conclusion

Responsible AI use requires more than producing polished content. Accuracy, privacy, copyright awareness, fairness, and human oversight are essential to creating a trustworthy personal portfolio.

The final website and supporting documents should reflect the student's real experience and the work actually completed.
