## 4. AI Hallucination Analysis

An AI hallucination occurs when an AI system produces unsupported or incorrect information.

Potential risks in this project include:

- Claiming a skill the student does not possess.
- Inventing achievements, experience, or certifications.
- Describing planned features as completed.
- Making unsupported claims about AI tools.
- Providing inaccurate statements about licensing or privacy.

Each risk must be checked against reliable evidence or the actual project implementation.

## 5. Example of a Content Correction

**Original draft:** "I am an expert in artificial intelligence and software development."

**Problem:** This statement overstates the experience established in the student's profile.

**Revised version:** "I am a second-year Information Technology student interested in software development and artificial intelligence."

**Reason:** The revised version communicates the student's interests without claiming unverified expertise.
